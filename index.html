Core investment thesis

Enhaus does not need to beat insurers at underwriting. It needs to beat the healthcare system at routing.

The highest-leverage asset is the moment before provider selection. If Enhaus becomes the trusted entry point, it can influence care setting, provider, price, modality and escalation. Those choices determine episode economics. Insurance then becomes an attached product for future uncertainty rather than the mechanism Enhaus relies on to finance a known current condition.

01 · Why this market

Large spend is useful. Fragmentation is the opportunity.

MSK care combines five conditions that favor a new front door: high frequency, enormous spend, confusing consumer choice, meaningful price dispersion, and many cases that can begin conservatively without starting in a high-cost setting. Direct-access rules have also reduced the structural need for a physician gatekeeper, although state and payer restrictions still matter.

$134.5B

Low-back and neck pain alone

A major U.S. health-spending study estimated $134.5B in low-back/neck spending in 2016 and another $129.8B across other MSK disorders. Enhaus does not need to capture the clinical spend itself to build value; it needs to influence the path through it.

The TAM is not “PT visits.” It is the transaction and decision layer around MSK episodes.
$1,700+

Insured consumers can still shop like cash payers

The 2026 minimum deductible for an HSA-qualified self-only HDHP is $1,700. That means many consumers have insurance yet still care deeply about transparent pricing for a routine episode.

A simple price can be a product feature even when insurance exists.
