Core thesis: Sell access after pain begins. Sell insurance before pain begins.

Care Now

A defined episode, not open-ended prepaid coverage.

Example: evaluation + 4 follow-ups + digital home program + virtual check-ins + negotiated add-on rate.

Injury Protect

Accident-only protection issued by a licensed carrier.

Example: $1,500–$3,000 routine recovery benefit for covered accidents, with pre-injury effective-date controls.

Embedded channels

Acquire broad populations before they know they will claim.

Sports leagues · gyms · outdoor memberships · events · employers · activity platforms.
