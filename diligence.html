What has to be true for Enhaus to become a large, profitable company?

This appendix converts the thesis into measurable assumptions: market penetration, provider economics, competitive positioning, regulatory structure, enterprise distribution, insurance attachment and a five-year operating scenario.

Diligence conclusion

Enhaus can become venture-scale if routing creates both economic spread and proprietary data.

The strongest model has three engines—care transactions, enterprise/navigation fees and carrier-backed accident distribution—sharing one intake, provider graph, matching system and outcomes dataset. The first financing milestone is not insurance premium volume; it is proof that Enhaus can acquire demand, route it safely and produce repeatable contribution margin.

04 · Regulatory architecture

Stay out of unnecessary regulated roles.

The clean launch separates navigation, clinical care and insurance. North Carolina law materially affects how Enhaus gets paid, especially on provider referrals and insurance administration.

Recommended early structure

  • Enhaus platform: non-diagnostic intake, navigation, pricing, payments and provider software.
  • Licensed clinicians: PT evaluation/treatment and escalation decisions.
  • Licensed insurance agency: sells/solicits carrier-backed accident products through licensed producers.
  • Carrier: holds underwriting risk and policy obligations.
  • External TPA/administrator: handles regulated claims/premium functions if necessary.

Key North Carolina constraints

  • Referral compensation: G.S. 90-401 prohibits a health-care provider from compensating someone for recommending/securing that provider’s employment by a patient.
  • Producer licensing: business entities selling/soliciting/negotiating insurance must be licensed and operate through licensed producers/DRLPs.
  • TPA: soliciting/effecting coverage, collecting premiums or adjusting/settling life/health claims can trigger TPA regulation.
  • MGA: substantial premium production plus underwriting/claims/reinsurance authority can trigger MGA rules.
Economic consequence: provider SaaS, consumer episode economics, employer fees and carrier-paid insurance compensation are cleaner starting points than simple provider-paid referral bounties.
