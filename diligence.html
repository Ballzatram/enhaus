What has to be true for Enhaus to become a large, profitable company?

The opportunity becomes underwritable when it is reduced to measurable economics: addressable episodes, provider spread, acquisition cost, network density, repeatable routing and an insurance attachment that adds margin without forcing Enhaus to retain early claims risk.

Investment conclusion

Three revenue engines can share one infrastructure layer.

Care transactions: contracted recovery episodes. Navigation/software: routing infrastructure sold to employers, providers and partners. Carrier-backed protection: producer/MGA/admin economics on future accident risk. All three compound the same intake, provider graph, routing and outcomes dataset.

03 · Insurance economics

Attach insurance; do not become the insurer first.

Aflac U.S. is a useful category benchmark. In 2025, benefits/claims were 47.3% of premiums and pretax adjusted margin was 20.6%; accident represented 18.9% of new U.S. annualized premium sales. This proves supplemental benefits can be profitable at scale, not that Enhaus will reproduce Aflac economics.

04 · Charlotte pilot

Build the dataset investors will underwrite.

The first year should prove the care engine, not maximize revenue. Every metric below directly attacks a core thesis risk.

12-month tests

  • 10–20 provider locations: prove network density.
  • 1,000–2,500 completed episodes: establish cohort economics.
  • <72h median first appointment: prove consumer utility.
  • Track intake→booking→completion: establish funnel conversion.
  • Track CAC by channel: compare DTC vs embedded/employer acquisition.
  • Track provider contribution: prove the spread after support/payment costs.

What unlocks the next round

  • Signed contracted rates, not hypothetical discounts.
  • Repeatable gross contribution after CAC.
  • Evidence providers value Enhaus demand.
  • Outcome and escalation data supporting routing quality.
  • One employer/league/gym embedded distribution pilot.
  • Carrier term sheet showing real accident-product economics.
The valuation inflection occurs when Enhaus can show that more routed volume improves both provider economics and the quality of its proprietary routing dataset.
