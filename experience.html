Get to the right care — without guessing.

Enhaus turns a messy healthcare decision into a guided transaction: understand the problem, check for reasons to escalate, choose a transparent care path, match a provider, and optionally protect against future accidental injuries.

What Enhaus does — and does not doEnhaus is a navigation concept, not a medical diagnosis. A production version would use licensed clinicians and deterministic safety policies for escalation. Demo providers, pricing and coverage are illustrative.
